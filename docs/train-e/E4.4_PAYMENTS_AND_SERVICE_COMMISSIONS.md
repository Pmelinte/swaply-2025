# Train E — E4.4 Payments & Service Commissions

## Scope

E4.4 defines the canonical payment contract for optional services surrounding a swap. The swap itself remains free and is never represented as a payable line item.

## Included

- service payment order contract;
- integer minor-unit money representation;
- ISO 4217 currency normalisation;
- category-specific commission policies;
- deterministic commission calculation;
- request-level idempotency keys;
- explicit payment and refund lifecycle;
- provider references and provider-event deduplication;
- owner-only reads;
- server-side-only writes;
- additive Supabase schema;
- regression tests.

## Payable categories

- courier;
- packaging;
- insurance;
- travel;
- accommodation;
- promotion;
- affiliate service.

No `swap`, `item`, `property`, `service-exchange`, `event-exchange`, wallet, escrow balance, credit or stored-value category is permitted by the contract.

## Authority boundary

Browser clients may read only their own payment orders. Creation, state transitions, provider references, refunds and event processing remain server-side responsibilities.

Provider confirmation is required before an order becomes `paid`, `payment-failed` or `refunded`. E4.5 will implement webhook signature verification, replay protection and reconciliation.

## Safety decisions

- no card data is stored;
- no provider is activated by this batch;
- no Production secret is added;
- no checkout route is migrated;
- no payment is initiated;
- no payout or revenue recognition is performed;
- no trust/rank benefit can be purchased;
- retries cannot create a second order for the same user, provider and idempotency key.

## Lifecycle

`draft → checkout-pending → payment-pending → paid → refund-pending → refunded`

Failure and cancellation branches are explicit. Invalid jumps such as `draft → paid` or `paid → refunded` are rejected.
