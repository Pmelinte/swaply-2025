# Train E / E3.5 — Cancel, Withdrawal & Dispute Authority

## Scope

E3.5 adds one server-side authority boundary for cancelling a multi-user exchange, withdrawing a participant before activation, and opening leg-level or exchange-level disputes.

## Contracts

- every action requires an authenticated active participant;
- observers and outsiders have no authority;
- every mutation is bound to the current agreement revision;
- cancellation is proposer-only and releases only active reservations;
- consumed reservation evidence is immutable;
- withdrawal is self-only and closes after atomic activation;
- withdrawal invalidates all acceptances for the previous revision;
- a remaining participant count below two cancels the exchange;
- a disputed leg blocks further lifecycle progression;
- completed exchanges remain terminal;
- repeated equivalent authority actions are idempotent;
- every accepted authority action creates participant-visible audit evidence.

## Explicit non-scope

- dispute resolution and moderator adjudication;
- ownership transfer;
- item deactivation;
- notifications;
- rewards, reputation, stories or payments;
- direct browser writes;
- automatic application of the Production migration.

## Gate

E3.5 is complete when the authority contract, migration safety tests, full CI, AI regression gate and Vercel Preview are green. Merge still requires Petru's explicit command.
