# Train E / E4.7 — Campaigns & Controlled Promotion

## Scope

E4.7 defines the canonical safety boundary for sponsored campaigns. It does not activate a Production advertising network, charge an advertiser or modify organic ranking.

## Delivery gates

A campaign may be delivered only when all of the following are true:

1. it is explicitly labelled `sponsored`;
2. moderation has approved it;
3. its status is approved or active;
4. the current time is inside the declared schedule;
5. its budget is not exhausted;
6. the per-user daily frequency cap is not reached;
7. country, locale, category and placement match contextual targeting;
8. the kill switch is not active.

Failure of any gate suppresses the campaign without affecting the underlying Swaply experience.

## Privacy and fairness

- advertisers cannot access impression-level viewer identity;
- targeting is contextual, not based on sensitive personal traits;
- sponsored content is visually and contractually separate from organic content;
- campaign spend cannot buy trust, reputation or rank;
- aggregate reporting may be added later from server-side evidence.

## Legacy finding

The repository already contains item boosts tied to Stripe and an ordering rule that can surface boosted items first. E4.7 does not activate or legitimise that legacy path. It creates the controlled campaign contract that any future promotion must use before Production activation.

## Safety boundary

- no real advertiser checkout;
- no Production ad provider;
- no personal-data export to advertisers;
- no hidden sponsored placement;
- no sensitive or discriminatory targeting;
- no trust-rank effects;
- no automatic migration of legacy boosts.
