# Train E / E4.6 — Commercial UI & Exchange Integration

## Scope

E4.6 introduces the user-facing safety boundary for optional commercial services surrounding a swap. It does not activate a payment provider or charge the swap itself.

## Rules

1. The swap price is always displayed and calculated as zero.
2. Courier, packaging, insurance, travel, accommodation and similar services remain optional.
3. Third-party, affiliate and sponsored origin must be disclosed visibly.
4. Service subtotal, Swaply commission and payable total must be separated before checkout.
5. A provider outage cannot block the underlying swap.
6. Removing or cancelling an optional service cannot cancel the swap.
7. Mixed-currency selections are rejected instead of producing an ambiguous total.
8. Payment history is ordered deterministically and may expose a provider receipt link when available.

## Integration delivered

- canonical commercial UI data contract;
- deterministic totals in integer minor units;
- explicit disclosure labels;
- optional-service and free-swap assertions;
- provider-unavailable fallback;
- checkout eligibility gate;
- order-history presentation contract;
- commercial disclosure boundary integrated into priced Exchange services;
- regression tests.

## Current boundary

Existing Exchange service components may save estimated prices in EUR. When a price is saved, the UI wraps that service in the E4.6 disclosure boundary and shows the service amount separately from the swap. Existing rows can also be passed through the `services` prop for the same treatment.

No real checkout, payment, refund, commission collection, provider secret or Production provider activation is introduced by this batch. The legacy escrow UI remains legacy and is not authorised for Production monetary custody.
