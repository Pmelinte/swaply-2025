## Confirmed defect

The public Privacy Policy named HuggingFace as the AI content-analysis processor, while the current server-side image-analysis route can use Groq, Google Gemini, or Hugging Face depending on configuration and provider availability.

This created a concrete mismatch between public privacy disclosure and executable implementation.

## Remediation

The Privacy page now adds an explicit AI-provider disclosure in the data-sharing section:

- image analysis is server-side;
- submitted image content may be processed by Groq, Google Gemini, or Hugging Face;
- the actual provider depends on configuration and availability;
- the disclosure does not claim that all providers are simultaneously active.

The legal revision badge is updated to `2026-08-08` because the public privacy meaning changed.

No AI-provider order, credentials, feature flags, model selection, paid-provider activation, Supabase schema, RLS, business logic, payment behavior, or GA/release authority is changed by this remediation.

## Analytics reconciliation

`ConditionalAnalytics` currently renders Google Analytics, when configured, and Vercel Analytics only after cookie consent is `accepted`. This is consistent with the public claim that analytics cookies/analytics are optional and consent-controlled.
