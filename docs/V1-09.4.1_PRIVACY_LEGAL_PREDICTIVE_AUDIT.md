## 4. Confirmed gaps or unresolved risks

### P1 — AI third-party processing disclosure is not yet proven

The executable V1-09 inventory requires public disclosure of an AI provider or third-party AI processing. Repository discovery found AI implementation and historical documentation, but the audit has not yet established that the current public Privacy/Terms surface contains an accurate disclosure.

No provider name should be added merely to satisfy the scanner. Disclosure must describe actual runtime behavior and preserve the boundary that paid AI activation is not authorised unless separately approved.

### P1 — Legal/privacy copy must be reconciled with runtime reality

The existence of Terms, Privacy and Cookies routes is not sign-off. Their claims must match current collection, processing, sharing, retention, deletion, cookies, analytics, dispute/report and commercial behavior.

### P1 — Exact-location handling needs a current end-to-end replay

Prior profile projection evidence is strong, but exact meeting/address data is a separate sensitive path. The final privacy gate must prove that exact location is never exposed through public projections and is available only to legitimate participants when required.

### P1 — Retention and deletion semantics are not yet proven

The UI exposes account deletion and GDPR requests, but final sign-off requires a category-by-category answer for messages, swaps, disputes/evidence, Stories, feedback, ledgers, audit/security records and other data that may require deletion, anonymisation or justified retention.

### P1 — Cookies/analytics policy may not be equivalent to implementation

A cookie policy route exists, including analytics and third-party sections. The next audit must inventory actual analytics/tracking code and cookies and compare them with the policy and consent behavior. Absence of analytics is also a valid outcome, but the public copy must not misrepresent it.

### P1 — Prohibited rules require policy/enforcement parity

A prohibited section exists in Terms. Final legal sign-off requires a deterministic policy inventory and proof that listing/moderation/report paths do not contradict it.

### P2 — Public legal dates are stale relative to the current release work

The current route implementations display fixed update dates from February/March 2026. If V1-09 changes legal/privacy meaning, the published revision date and localized legal content must be reconciled as part of the legal remediation rather than silently left stale.

## 5. Areas not to reopen without evidence of regression

The predictive audit does not justify speculative rewrites of already-closed authority systems. The following have strong prior evidence and should be replayed first, then modified only on confirmed failure:

- public/private profile projection;
- participant-only message authority;
- Stories consent/privacy authority;
- report/block authority;
- dispute authority.
