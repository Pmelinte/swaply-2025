## Why Swap Medical Equipment?

The cost of medical equipment in many countries is staggering. A standard wheelchair can cost anywhere from $200 to $2,000 or more. Blood pressure monitors, crutches, knee braces, and glucose monitors all carry price tags that add up quickly, especially when insurance does not cover them or when you need them temporarily. Many people purchase these items for a short recovery period and then have no further use for them. Meanwhile, someone else in your community may desperately need the exact device sitting in your garage.

Swapping medical equipment on Swaply creates a cycle of care. You give your unused wheelchair to someone recovering from surgery, and in return, you receive something you actually need. It is practical, sustainable, and genuinely impactful. According to various healthcare studies, the average household has at least one piece of unused medical equipment at any given time. That is an enormous pool of resources waiting to be shared.

## What Medical Equipment Can You Swap on Swaply?

Not all medical equipment is created equal when it comes to swapping. Swaply allows the trading of certain categories of devices that are safe to transfer between users when properly cleaned and maintained. Here is what you can list:

### Mobility Aids

- **Manual wheelchairs** in good working condition with functional brakes and wheels
- **Crutches** (underarm and forearm styles) that are adjustable and structurally sound
- **Walking canes** and **quad canes** without cracks or damage
- **Knee scooters** and **rollators** with working brakes and intact frames
- **Transfer boards** and **grab bars** that have not been permanently installed

### Monitoring Devices

- **Blood pressure monitors** (digital, upper arm or wrist models)
- **Glucometers** and blood glucose monitors (device only, not test strips)
- **Pulse oximeters** in working condition
- **Digital thermometers** that have been properly sanitized
- **Peak flow meters** for respiratory monitoring

### Recovery and Support Equipment

- **Ankle, knee, and wrist braces** that are clean and structurally intact
- **Slings** and **support wraps** in good condition
- **TENS units** and electrotherapy devices with working leads
- **Ice therapy machines** and cold compression units
- **Elevated toilet seats** and **shower chairs** that are sanitized

## What Medical Equipment Cannot Be Traded

Swaply takes safety seriously, and certain categories of medical equipment are strictly prohibited from being listed on the platform. These restrictions exist to protect both traders and comply with healthcare regulations. The following items are not allowed:

- **Prescription medical devices** that require a doctor's authorization to use
- **CPAP and BiPAP machines** due to hygiene concerns and prescription requirements
- **Hearing aids** that are individually calibrated and prescribed
- **Powered wheelchairs** and scooters that require professional fitting
- **Prosthetic limbs** and custom orthotics
- **Syringes, needles, and sharps** of any kind
- **Used test strips** or any single-use diagnostic consumables
- **Expired medications** or any pharmaceutical products
- **Oxygen tanks** and oxygen concentrators due to safety hazards
- **Implantable devices** of any type
- **Breast pumps** that are not designed for multi-user use
- **Contact lenses** and prescription eyewear

If you are unsure whether a specific item is allowed, contact the Swaply support team before listing it. We review flagged listings daily and can provide guidance within 24 hours.

## Hygiene Requirements for Medical Equipment Swaps

Hygiene is non-negotiable when it comes to medical equipment. Every item listed on Swaply must meet strict cleanliness standards before it can be swapped. Here is what we require:

### Before Listing

1. **Disinfect all surfaces** using hospital-grade disinfectant wipes or a solution of 70% isopropyl alcohol
2. **Remove all personal data** from digital devices like blood pressure monitors and glucometers
3. **Replace any consumable parts** such as cuffs, pads, or covers that cannot be fully sanitized
4. **Inspect for damage** including cracks, rust, fraying, or broken mechanisms
5. **Test functionality** to confirm the device works as intended

### Photography Standards

When photographing medical equipment for your listing, include:

- A full shot of the device from multiple angles
- Close-ups of any wear, scratches, or cosmetic imperfections
- A photo of the device powered on (for electronic equipment) to prove it works
- A photo of any included accessories, manuals, or replacement parts

### At the Swap

- Bring disinfectant wipes for a final cleaning at the point of exchange
- Allow the recipient to test the equipment before completing the swap
- Provide any user manuals, charging cables, or accessories that came with the device
- Share relevant information about battery life, maintenance schedules, or known quirks

## Condition Verification and Grading

Swaply uses a condition grading system for medical equipment that is more detailed than our standard item grading. When listing medical equipment, you must select one of the following conditions:

- **Like New**: Used fewer than five times, no visible wear, all original parts and packaging included
- **Excellent**: Fully functional with minor cosmetic wear, all essential parts included
- **Good**: Fully functional with moderate cosmetic wear, may be missing non-essential accessories
- **Fair**: Functional but shows significant wear, may require minor maintenance or replacement parts

Items rated below "Fair" should not be listed. Medical equipment must always be functional and safe to use. If a device has any defect that could compromise user safety, it must not be traded on Swaply under any circumstances.

## Tips for a Successful Medical Equipment Swap

Beyond the requirements listed above, here are some best practices that experienced Swaply traders follow when swapping medical equipment:

- **Be transparent about usage history**: Let the other party know how long you used the device, how often, and for what purpose
- **Include the original box and manuals** whenever possible, as these add value and help the recipient use the device correctly
- **Set realistic expectations**: Medical equipment depreciates, so price your item accordingly and be open to a range of swap offers
- **Respond promptly to questions**: Someone seeking medical equipment often needs it urgently, so fast communication matters
- **Consider donating** if you cannot find a swap partner and the equipment is still in good condition

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I swap medical equipment that has been recalled?

No. Any medical equipment that has been subject to a manufacturer recall must not be listed on Swaply. Check the manufacturer's website or your country's medical device recall database before listing. If a recall is issued after you have listed an item, remove the listing immediately and notify any interested parties.

### Do I need to provide proof that the device works?

Yes. Swaply requires that all electronic medical devices be photographed in a powered-on state to demonstrate functionality. For non-electronic items like crutches or braces, clear photos showing structural integrity are sufficient. If a potential swap partner requests a video demonstration, we encourage you to provide one.

### Can I swap medical equipment across state or national borders?

This depends on the regulations in your jurisdiction. Some medical devices are subject to import and export restrictions. Swaply recommends limiting medical equipment swaps to local or regional exchanges to avoid regulatory complications. Always check local laws before shipping medical devices.

### What if the equipment breaks after the swap?

Swaply swaps are conducted on an as-is basis once both parties confirm the exchange. However, if you believe the other party misrepresented the condition of the equipment, you can file a dispute through Swaply's resolution center within seven days of the swap. Our team will review the evidence and mediate a fair outcome.

### Are batteries and consumables included in a swap?

This varies by listing. Some traders include fresh batteries or replacement parts, while others list the device only. Always read the listing description carefully and ask the seller to clarify what is included before committing to a swap. If batteries or consumables are not included, factor in the cost of purchasing them separately.

### How do I know if a blood pressure monitor is accurate?

We recommend testing any swapped blood pressure monitor against a reading taken at a pharmacy or doctor's office. If the readings differ significantly, the device may need recalibration or replacement. Never rely solely on a swapped monitoring device for critical health decisions without verifying its accuracy first.
