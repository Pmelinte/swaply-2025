## What Travel Experiences Can You Swap?

The world of tradable travel experiences is broader than most people realize. Here is a breakdown of what you can list on Swaply:

### Transferable Flight Tickets and Vouchers

Not all flight tickets can be transferred, but many can. Airlines increasingly offer transferable booking credits, open tickets, and vouchers that are perfect for swapping. Here is what qualifies:

- **Airline vouchers and credits** issued by the airline for cancellations or delays
- **Open-date tickets** that allow the name to be changed before a specific deadline
- **Gift cards** for airlines that can be applied to any booking
- **Companion passes** and buddy passes that allow you to bring someone along on a flight
- **Frequent flyer miles** on programs that allow point transfers between members

Before listing any flight-related item, verify with the airline that the ticket, voucher, or credit is genuinely transferable. Swaply requires that you include the transfer policy details in your listing description so the other party knows exactly what they are getting.

### Hotel Reservations and Stays

Hotels are often more flexible than airlines when it comes to transfers. Many reservations can be modified to change the guest name, especially when booked through third-party platforms. Tradable hotel experiences include:

- **Prepaid hotel reservations** with flexible name-change policies
- **Hotel gift cards** and credit vouchers from major chains
- **Timeshare weeks** that allow guest passes or name transfers
- **Resort credits** and spa packages that are not tied to a specific individual
- **Loyalty program points** on hotel programs that permit transfers

When listing a hotel reservation, always include the check-in and check-out dates, the hotel name and location, the room type, whether breakfast or other amenities are included, and the cancellation policy. Transparency is key to building trust and closing the swap quickly.

## How to Verify Transferability

The single most important step in trading travel experiences is verifying that the item can actually be transferred to another person. Here is a step-by-step process:

1. **Check the booking confirmation** for any language about transfers, name changes, or restrictions
2. **Contact the provider directly** (airline, hotel, event venue) and ask whether the booking can be transferred to a different name
3. **Ask about fees**: Some providers allow transfers but charge a fee, which should be disclosed in your listing
4. **Get written confirmation** via email or chat that the transfer is permitted
5. **Screenshot the transfer policy** and include it in your Swaply listing for transparency
6. **Note any deadlines**: Many transfers must be completed a certain number of days before the event or travel date

If a provider does not allow transfers under any circumstances, do not list the item on Swaply. Attempting to swap a non-transferable booking is against our terms of service and could result in account suspension.

## Tips for Successful Travel Experience Swaps

Trading travel experiences requires a bit more diligence than swapping physical goods. Here are proven strategies from experienced Swaply traders:

- **List early**: Travel experiences lose value as the date approaches. List your item as soon as you know you will not use it
- **Be detailed in your description**: Include dates, locations, inclusions, exclusions, and any restrictions. The more information you provide, the faster you will find a match
- **Price your swap realistically**: Research what the experience is worth on the open market and set your swap expectations accordingly
- **Respond quickly**: Travel swaps are time-sensitive. A slow response could mean the other party finds an alternative
- **Use Swaply's escrow system**: For high-value travel swaps, use Swaply's built-in verification to protect both parties
- **Include transfer instructions**: Explain exactly how the transfer will work so the other party knows what to expect
- **Keep communication on-platform**: This protects both parties and creates a record in case of disputes

## Understanding the Risks

While travel experience swaps can be incredibly rewarding, they come with risks that you should understand:

- **Date sensitivity**: Travel items expire. A hotel reservation for next weekend has very different urgency than one six months away
- **Transfer complications**: Even when a provider confirms transferability, technical issues can arise during the actual transfer process
- **Value fluctuations**: Flight prices and hotel rates change constantly, which can affect the perceived value of your listing
- **No-show risk**: If the other party does not follow through on their end of the swap after you have transferred the travel experience, you could be left empty-handed

Swaply's dispute resolution system is designed to handle these scenarios, but prevention is always better than cure. Verify everything twice, communicate openly, and use the platform's built-in protections.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I swap international flight tickets on Swaply?

Yes, as long as the ticket or voucher is genuinely transferable. International flights often have stricter transfer policies than domestic ones, so verify with the airline before listing. Include any visa or entry requirements in your listing description so the other party can confirm they are eligible to travel.

### What happens if the hotel cancels my reservation after I swap it?

If the provider cancels the booking after the swap has been completed, contact Swaply's support team immediately. We will mediate between both parties and work toward a fair resolution, which may include reversing the swap or arranging an alternative compensation.

### How do I determine the value of my travel experience for swapping?

Check the current market price for the same or similar experience. For flights, look at the current booking price on the airline's website. For hotels, check the nightly rate. For events, see what tickets are selling for on resale platforms. Your swap expectations should be roughly equivalent to the current market value.

### Can I swap a travel experience for a non-travel item?

Absolutely. That is one of the things that makes Swaply unique. You can swap a hotel stay for electronics, concert tickets for furniture, or flight vouchers for sporting equipment. The only requirement is that both parties agree the swap is fair.

### What if the event gets cancelled after I swap the tickets?

If the event organizer cancels and issues refunds, the refund typically goes to the original purchaser. You would need to coordinate with the person you swapped with to ensure they receive the refund value. Swaply's support team can help mediate this process if needed.

### Are there any travel experiences that cannot be swapped on Swaply?

Yes. Non-transferable bookings, expired vouchers, tickets with names that cannot be changed, and any travel documents that are restricted by law from being transferred to another person are not allowed on the platform. When in doubt, check with the provider and Swaply's support team.
