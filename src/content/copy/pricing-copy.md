## FAQ

### 1. Can I swap without paying?

Absolutely. Swaply's core swapping experience is completely free and always will be. You can list items, receive AI-powered matches, chat with other swappers, and complete trades without ever paying a cent. The Starter plan is not a trial — it's a permanent free tier with no expiration date. Paid plans simply unlock additional tools for users who want to swap more actively.

### 2. What are tokens?

Tokens are Swaply's in-app currency used for optional premium actions like boosting a listing to the top of search results, purchasing featured placement on the homepage, or unlocking special filters. Premium and Platinum members receive a monthly token allocation included in their plan. Free users can earn tokens by completing swaps, leaving reviews, and referring friends.

### 3. Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your Premium or Platinum subscription at any time with no cancellation fees, no penalties, and no questions asked. When you cancel, you'll continue to have access to your paid features until the end of your current billing cycle. After that, your account will automatically revert to the free Starter plan with all your data and history intact.

### 4. Is there a free trial?

We don't offer a traditional free trial because our Starter plan is permanently free with no time limit. You can use Swaply indefinitely on the free plan and upgrade only when you feel the need for additional features. There's no pressure, no countdown timer, and no credit card required to get started. Try the free plan, and upgrade whenever it makes sense for you.

### 5. Do you take commission on swaps?

Never. Swaply does not take a percentage, commission, or transaction fee on any swap — regardless of your plan. When you trade a laptop for a camera, both parties keep 100% of the value. Our revenue comes from optional Premium and Platinum subscriptions, not from taxing your trades. Fair swapping means the value stays with the swappers.
