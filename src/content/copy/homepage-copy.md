## How It Works

### 1. List What You Can Offer

Create a listing with clear information about what you are offering and what you would consider in exchange.

### 2. Discover Possible Matches

Browse listings and review available matching signals. Suggestions can help discovery, but they are not guarantees and should not be described as universally AI-generated unless current Production evidence demonstrates that behavior.

### 3. Chat & Agree

Use in-app communication to discuss scope, timing, condition and logistics with the other participant.

### 4. Complete & Review

Record the agreed exchange flow and leave feedback where the applicable workflow supports it.

## Why Swaply

### Direct Exchange

Swaply is designed around voluntary user-to-user exchange. Swaply does not charge a Swaply commission on the basic swap itself. Optional third-party services may have separate costs if they are offered.

### Global-First Design

Swaply contains a 43-locale interface configuration. This should not be described as proof that every message, listing, provider or workflow is automatically translated in every locale.

### Trust Controls

Swaply provides platform controls such as reporting, blocking, disputes and reputation signals where implemented. Account or email verification must not be described as identity-document verification.

### Provider Transparency

Courier, payment, escrow, insurance and travel-provider foundations are labelled according to their real operational state. A provider adapter or future plan is not described as a live integration.

### Sustainability

Swaply supports reuse and direct exchange as part of a circular-economy approach. Environmental impact should not be expressed as a measured statistic unless a verified measurement source exists.
