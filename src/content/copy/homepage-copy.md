## How It Works — 4 Steps

### 1. List Your Item

Upload a photo, write a short description, and set your swap preferences. The better your listing, the faster you'll find the perfect match.

### 2. Get Matched by AI

Our smart matching algorithm analyzes thousands of listings to find the most compatible swaps for you. You'll receive personalized match suggestions ranked by relevance, location, and value.

### 3. Chat & Agree

Use our secure in-app messaging to discuss details, ask questions, and agree on terms. Everything stays on-platform so both parties are protected throughout the negotiation.

### 4. Swap & Review

Meet locally or ship your items using one of our 200+ courier partners. After the swap, leave a review to help build trust across the community.

## Why Swaply — 6 Benefits

### 1. Zero Fees

Swaply will never charge you a commission, transaction fee, or hidden cost on your swaps. Trading is free — the way bartering was always meant to be. Your items, your trades, your value.

### 2. AI-Powered Matching

Our intelligent algorithm doesn't just match keywords — it understands value, category compatibility, and user preferences. The more you swap, the smarter your matches become, delivering increasingly relevant suggestions over time.

### 3. 43 Languages

Swap with anyone in the world without language barriers. Our auto-translation feature handles messages, listings, and reviews in 43 languages so you can negotiate confidently with partners across the globe.

### 4. Safe & Verified

Every user has a trust score built from verified identity, swap history, and community reviews. Our optional escrow service protects both parties during shipping, and identity verification ensures you're always dealing with real people.

### 5. Global Reach

Swaply operates in over 100 countries with 200+ integrated courier partners for seamless cross-border shipping. Whether your swap partner is across the street or across the ocean, we make logistics simple and affordable.

### 6. Eco-Friendly

Every swap keeps one more item out of a landfill. By choosing to trade instead of buy new, you're participating in the circular economy and actively reducing waste. Swaply tracks your environmental impact so you can see the difference you're making.
