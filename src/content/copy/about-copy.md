# Swaply — About Page Copy

## Hero

**Headline:** About Swaply

**Subheadline:** Swaply is a web platform for people who want to discover and arrange voluntary exchanges of objects, properties, services and events.

---

## Our Story

Swaply was founded in Romania in 2025 from the idea that technology can make direct exchange easier to discover and organize.

The product is being developed with a global-first approach, but public claims are deliberately limited to facts and capabilities that can be demonstrated.

Swaply does not publish a user count, active-country count, courier count or other scale statistic without a documented and reproducible source.

---

## Our Mission

**Make voluntary exchange easier to discover, discuss and complete.**

Swaply is designed to help people describe what they can offer, discover possible exchanges and communicate about the terms of an exchange.

---

## Product Truth

Swaply distinguishes between four important states:

- **Live:** demonstrated operational behavior;
- **Foundation:** code, routes, schema or UI foundations that do not prove an operational service;
- **Demo:** illustrative or sample behavior that must be explicitly labelled;
- **Disabled / Planned:** provider or product capabilities that are not currently offered as live services.

A foundation, demo or disabled provider is never described as live.

---

## Trust and Safety

Swaply includes reporting, blocking, dispute and reputation controls where implemented. Account verification or email verification must not be presented as identity-document verification.

Escrow, insurance and other third-party protection services must not be described as live unless they are separately activated, legally reviewed and verified in Production.

---

## Multilingual Product

The application contains a 43-locale interface configuration. This means the product is structured for multilingual use; it does not mean that every listing, message, provider or workflow is automatically translated in every language.

---

## Providers and Logistics

Swaply may contain provider adapters, deep-link concepts, integration routes or future commercial plans. These foundations do not establish an active partnership or integration by themselves.

Each courier, payment, travel, escrow, insurance or other provider must be verified individually before being described publicly as operational.

---

## Pricing

Swaply does not charge a Swaply commission on the basic user-to-user swap itself. Optional third-party services may have separate costs if offered.

No new Premium or Business subscription price should be advertised until the commercial plan, provider configuration, checkout flow and entitlements are approved and verified in Production.

---

## Public Metrics

No unverified scale metric is part of the public About copy. Counts for users, listings, countries, partners or couriers require a documented source before publication.
