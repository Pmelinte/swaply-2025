**Write an honest description.** Include the brand, condition, age, and any flaws. Transparency builds trust, and trusted profiles get matched first. Be specific: "iPhone 12, 64GB, minor scratch on back, battery health 87%" beats "old phone, works fine."

**Choose your range.** Decide if you want to swap locally, nationally, or internationally. Local swaps are great for meetups; international swaps use our integrated shipping with 200+ courier partners.

Your first listing takes less than two minutes. And once it's live, our AI matching engine gets to work immediately — scanning thousands of listings to find the perfect swap for you.

The sooner you list, the sooner you swap.

## Email 7: Review Request (Post-swap)

**Subject:** How was your swap with {{partner_name}}?
**Preview text:** Your feedback helps the whole community.

**Body:**

Hi {{first_name}},

You recently completed a swap with **{{partner_name}}**, and we'd love to hear how it went.

Your review matters more than you might think. Here's why:

**It builds trust.** Every review contributes to your partner's Trust Score — the number that helps other swappers decide who to trade with. A positive review can be the difference between someone hesitating and someone saying "yes" to a swap. And honest feedback about problems helps the community stay safe and fair.

**It improves matching.** Our AI uses review data to refine its matching algorithm. When you report that a swap went smoothly, that information helps us make better suggestions for everyone. When you flag an issue, we investigate and learn from it.

**It takes less than a minute.** Here's all we're asking:

1. **Rate the swap** from 1 to 5 stars. Was the item as described? Was communication smooth? Was the handoff or shipping handled well?
2. **Write a short review.** Even one or two sentences help. Something like: "Item was exactly as described, fast shipping, great communication" gives future swappers the confidence they need.
3. **Rate specific aspects** (optional). You can separately rate item accuracy, communication quality, packaging, and timeliness.

**Your review is visible on your partner's profile** and contributes to their overall Trust Score. Similarly, when your swap partners review you, it builds your own reputation and helps you get better matches in the future.

Swappers who consistently leave reviews are 60% more likely to receive reviews in return — and profiles with reviews get matched 2x faster.

Help us build a community where everyone swaps with confidence.

**Primary CTA:** Write My Review

**Footer note:** Reviews are moderated for abuse but never edited. Your honest opinion is always welcome. Having an issue with this swap? Contact Support.
