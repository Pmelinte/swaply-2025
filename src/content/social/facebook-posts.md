## Post 4 — Tip of the Week

💡 SWAPLY TIP OF THE WEEK 💡

Want more swap requests? Here's the secret: PHOTOS. 📸

Listings with 3+ high-quality photos get 4x more swap offers than listings with just one blurry pic.

Here's how to take a great listing photo:
1. 📱 Use natural light — stand near a window
2. 🧹 Clear the background — let your item be the star
3. 📐 Take one straight-on shot + one detail shot + one "in use" shot
4. ✨ Clean the item first — it shows you care

It takes 5 extra minutes and it makes a HUGE difference. Try it on your next listing!

What items have you been meaning to list? Tell us below! 👇
