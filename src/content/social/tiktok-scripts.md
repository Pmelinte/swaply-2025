[Cut to clean, bright setting — creator at a table with a few items in front of them]
**VOICEOVER:** "Okay so here's the thing about bartering. For thousands of years, it was literally how people got things. You had something I needed, I had something you needed, we traded. Done."

[Creator picks up phone]
**VOICEOVER:** "Then money came along and we forgot about it. But money has problems — inflation, fees, inequality. Bartering has none of that. It's just pure, direct value exchange."

[Cut back to creator]
**VOICEOVER:** "Here's why it works better than selling: when you sell, you get cash, then you have to go spend that cash. Extra steps, extra friction. When you swap, you go directly from something you don't want to something you do. No middle step. Just instant value."

**VOICEOVER:** "And because both of you need the trade to happen, people actually show up. No ghosting. No lowball offers. Just two people with a deal that works for both."

## Script 5 — "The Circular Economy Explained in 60 Seconds"

**HOOK (0–3 seconds)**
[Fast-cut graphics: factory → store → home → trash → factory loop shown as a broken cycle]
**ON SCREEN TEXT:** "The way we shop is broken. Here's the fix."
**VOICEOVER:** "We have a consumption problem. But the solution is simpler than you think."

---

**CONTENT (3–50 seconds)**
[Clean whiteboard animation style or simple graphic cuts]
**VOICEOVER:** "Right now, the economy works like this: something gets made, you buy it, you use it for a while, you throw it away. Then someone makes another one. Buy, use, trash. Repeat forever."

[Graph showing rising waste over time]
**VOICEOVER:** "The problem? We're drowning in the 'trash' part. Americans throw away $450 billion in usable goods every year. That's not a waste problem — that's a distribution problem. The right stuff isn't getting to the right people."

[Animation shifts to circular flow: person A has item → swaps with person B → person B passes to person C → item stays in use]
**VOICEOVER:** "The circular economy fixes this. Instead of buy-use-trash, it's use-swap-use again. Items stay in circulation. Value stays in the community. The planet gets a break."

[Cut to Swaply app interface]
**VOICEOVER:** "Swaply is a circular economy platform for everyday people. You don't need to understand supply chains or carbon offsets. You just need to look at what you have, find someone who needs it, and swap for something you actually want."

[Quick montage of happy swappers, items changing hands, diverse community]
**VOICEOVER:** "Every swap is a vote for a better model. A model where we take care of what we make, and take care of each other."

**VOICEOVER:** "Be part of the solution. Download Swaply. Make one swap this week. It's free, it's fast, and it actually feels good. Link in bio."
